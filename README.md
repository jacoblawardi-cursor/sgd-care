# SGD Care - Premium Home Maintenance & Property Care

**SGD Care** is a specialized property maintenance and home care business unit spun off from **SGD Construction** (PT Sunggiardi Prima Konstruksi).

This website is powered by **WordPress** featuring a custom, bespoke **Genshin Green (Dendro)** theme engineered for luxury aesthetics, fast performance, and high conversion.

---

## Brand & Visual System: "Genshin Green Theme"
- **Emerald & Jade Palette**: Deep forest green (`#0b4d36`, `#063324`), luminous mint glow (`#10b981`, `#34d399`), pale dew backgrounds (`#f0fdf4`, `#ecfdf5`), and Genshin luxury gold star accents (`#f59e0b`).
- **Typography**: Plus Jakarta Sans, Inter, and Cinzel.
- **Micro-interactions**: Floating glowing cards, interactive service selector, WhatsApp diagnostic estimation bridge.

---

## Core Services & Features
1. **Quick Care Diagnostic & Cost Estimator**: Interactive calculator that estimates initial costs based on service type, property category, and square meters, then automatically formats a structured WhatsApp message to dispatch a technician.
2. **6 Care Pillars**:
   - Roof Leak Detection & Waterproofing (Infrared / Thermal Diagnostic)
   - AC & HVAC Maintenance (Inverter, Split, Cassette)
   - MEP & Electrical Safety Diagnostics (Panel load balance, grounding)
   - Pipe, Sanitary & Water Pressure Maintenance
   - Wood Finishing & Architectural Touch-Up
   - Comprehensive Home Health Check
3. **Care Subscription Plans**:
   - *Essential Care*
   - *Comfort Living* (Featured)
   - *Total Estate Care* (VIP)
4. **WhatsApp Emergency Integration**: Instant direct dispatch with one-click float button.
5. **SGD Construction Pedigree**: Leverages contractor-grade engineering standards for residential home care.

## Local Setup (MAMP)
1. Ensure Apache and MySQL are running in MAMP.
2. Create database `sgd_care` via phpMyAdmin (`http://localhost/phpmyadmin` or `http://localhost:8888/phpmyadmin`).
3. Database configuration is pre-configured in `wp-config.php`:
   - **DB Name**: `sgd_care`
   - **DB User**: `root`
   - **DB Password**: `root`
   - **DB Host**: `localhost`
4. Access the site via your browser at `http://localhost/sgd-care` (or configured MAMP port).
